About Me

"Translating technical risk into decisions people can act on — for engineers, auditors, and executives alike."

I'm Harshvardhan Singh, a Technology Risk & Controls consultant at EY, with two years assessing enterprise systems across India's top BFSI institutions — translating thousands of technical risk observations into audit-ready findings and regulator-aligned remediation roadmaps.

My day-to-day work spans application, API, cloud, mobile, and database risk domains, mapped against frameworks like the RBI Cybersecurity Framework, ISO 27001, NIST CSF, and DPDPA — bridging the gap between engineering teams and non-technical stakeholders.

I hold the ISO/IEC 42001:2023 AI Management Systems Lead Implementer certification and am a Certified LLM Security Professional, and I'm extending that governance foundation into offensive AI/red-team risk through the CRTP certification (in progress).

I'm deliberately building toward AI Security and Adversarial ML research — combining a controls-and-governance foundation with hands-on technical depth in how AI systems actually fail.
